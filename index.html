Finance · Build vs Buy

What I'd have built here — vs Upgrade AI

Same four jobs. One keeps finance inside our own stack (Ziff); the other is a $50k/yr third-party SaaS finance signed on 6/2.

Claude Finance — built here Upgrade AI “Specter” — signed
Cost~$0 recurring — Claude tokens on our own infra (Mac Mini / Vercel)$4,165/mo ≈ $50k/yr, auto-renews
3-year cost~token spend only≈ $150k
Where it livesInside Ziff — one login, one data layer, next to Travel + Spend IntelligenceTheir third-party platform — finance re-siloed off our stack
The AI engineClaude direct (Vercel AI Gateway)Wrapper on OpenAI/Anthropic/Gemini/Mistral — i.e. reselling us Claude
Sage IntacctDirect API integration we ownNative (their plumbing)
AsanaNone — Ziff workflows (Asana is being sunset)Integrates Asana — deepens the tool we're killing
Data & IPOurs — system of record, feeds the rest of the appThey own the engine; outputs assigned to us
Time to liveBuild + maintain — finance was our gap, never startedLive 7/15, low lift — they shipped while we sat
ComplianceWe own controls (no SOC 2 cert of our own yet)SOC 2 Type II (verify the report)
Exit / lock-inNo contract — it's our code1-yr term, 30-day non-renewal out
Bottom line: building it here wins on cost, ownership, stack-fit, and strategy (kill SaaS, sunset Asana). Buying wins on speed-to-live, an existing SOC 2, and zero maintenance. Upgrade AI only won this lane because finance was the gap we never started — and since Specter runs on Claude anyway, we'd be building on the same engine, just owning it instead of renting it at $50k/yr.
